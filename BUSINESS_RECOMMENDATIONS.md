# Business Recommendations

Each recommendation is tied to a result in the (synthetic) data. In a real business, test each idea before acting.

| # | Recommendation | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reduce dependence on Electronics and one hero product.** Grow Appliances, Home & Kitchen and Furniture, and watch supply and pricing of the top smartwatch closely. | Electronics is ~50% of item revenue (₹122.36 Cr of ₹244.99 Cr); one product earns ₹52.07 Cr (~21%). |
| 2 | **Prepare for the Q4 peak.** Build stock, staffing and delivery capacity from October. | Q4 was the strongest quarter in 2023, 2024 and 2025 (31.1%, 30.5%, 46.4% of annual revenue). December 2025 is the top month (₹24.78 Cr). |
| 3 | **Run win-back campaigns for churned and at-risk customers.** | 13,365 customers (53.5%) are labelled Churned and 2,101 At Risk. |
| 4 | **Reward the top spenders.** A loyalty or early-access programme for the highest-value buyers. | Top 10% of buyers bring 38.6% of order value; 82.4% of buyers already order more than once. |
| 5 | **Fix delivery promises.** Review Same-Day and Standard service levels and carriers. | 42.8% of delivered shipments are late; Same-Day 57.4% and Standard 54.8% late, versus Next-Day 10.8%. |
| 6 | **Reduce Fashion and Footwear returns.** Better size guides and photos; check damaged-item handling. | Fashion has the most return records (3,185); Footwear 8.5% and Fashion 7.4% return records per item sold; Size issue is the top reason (2,201). |
| 7 | **Investigate the late-2025 return surge.** | Monthly return records rose from about 300 to 740, 770 and 874 in Oct to Dec 2025. |
| 8 | **Cut payment failures.** Check payment gateway errors and offer UPI as the default. | 5,068 failed payments (5.1%); UPI is used for 32.0% of payments. |
| 9 | **Rebalance marketing with a proper test.** Move budget toward channels with higher attributed revenue per rupee, then run holdout tests before trusting them. | Attributed revenue per rupee of cost: Organic Search about 5.3x, Paid Search 1.9x, Facebook 1.1x. These are attribution, not incremental, figures. |
| 10 | **Grow low-penetration states.** Test local offers in Bihar. | Bihar: ₹4.69 Cr and 431 customers versus Maharashtra ₹35.99 Cr and 3,541 customers. |
| 11 | **Fix data consistency.** Align order status with return records and payment refunds. | See unresolved discrepancies in LIMITATIONS_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md. |
